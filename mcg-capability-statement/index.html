Strategic Planning

A strategy is only as sound as the organization’s ability to carry it out. MCG facilitates planning with executive teams and boards that ties priorities to structure, roles, and accountable owners, then stays with the work through execution and review.

Organizational Capacity Building

Capacity is an organization’s ability to perform under normal conditions and under strain. MCG applies its proprietary CATALYST Framework to assess where structure, talent, and culture support the mission, where they constrain it, and what it will take to close the gap.

Leadership Development

What an enterprise can achieve depends on the people who lead it and the potential of those they lead. MCG’s leadership work draws on the partners’ experience assessing, coaching, and developing leaders in demanding institutions.

  • Executive coaching: confidential, one-on-one work with CEOs, executives, and rising leaders.
  • Leader assessment: structured insight that informs selection, placement, and development.
  • Succession planning: preparing the next generation of leaders before the need is urgent.
  • Senior team effectiveness: aligning leadership teams on roles, decisions, and ways of working.
